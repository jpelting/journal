Communities are a space for sharing prayer requests and concerns with a trusted group of people. By participating in one, you agree to:

  • Use the community for sharing prayer requests and concerns, not for unrelated purposes.
  • Understand that anything you share - including "immediate" and "scheduled" prayer requests - is visible to other members of the community, and may be sent to them by email, unless you mark it anonymous.
  • Treat other members' shared requests and concerns as confidential, and not to be repeated outside the community.
  • Follow the community admin's guidance and the community's standards of respectful conduct.

The admin who created a community is responsible for moderating its membership and content. Report any concerns about how a community is being used to them directly.
