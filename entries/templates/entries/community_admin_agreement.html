Community Admin Agreement

The Community feature exists to help members share prayer concerns and requests with a trusted group of people. When you create a community, you become its admin, and by doing so you agree to:

  • Use the community for sharing prayer requests and concerns, not for unrelated purposes.
  • Review join requests thoughtfully, approving only people who should reasonably be part of this community.
  • Manage the community's membership - including removing members if needed - to keep the space safe and appropriate.
  • Take responsibility for upholding respectful standards of conduct among members.

As admin, you are the sole moderator of this community. The Wax Tablet does not actively monitor community content - that responsibility rests with you.
